02 / Just:DJ

Just DJ.

Just:DJ is Rick Duncan's vinyl-powered DJ and broadcast project — combining the physical performance of turntables with modern music, professional live production and distinctive visual presentation.

Built around Technics turntables and vinyl/DVS, the format keeps DJing tactile: manual mixing, pitch control, scratching and hands on the records rather than sync-led performance.

The music moves across house, techno, trance, drum & bass, jungle, hardcore and the spaces between them. The common thread is energy, strong programming and a DJ performance that's as interesting to watch as it is to hear.

Just:DJ is available for clubs, festivals, guest broadcasts, radio, live streams, collaborations and selected events.

03 / Vinyl

Old-school
interface. No old-school
limits.

Turntables are not a visual prop. They are the performance interface.

Rick uses Technics turntables with vinyl and DVS/control vinyl, combining the physical feel and manual control of traditional DJing with access to a modern digital catalogue.

Pitch, timing, phrasing, scratching and record control remain hands-on. The technology expands the music library without replacing the DJ technique.

  • Serato DVS

05 / Experience

Rick Duncan has spent more than three decades mixing on turntables, developing a style built around timing, selection, control and performance rather than automation.

Today that experience sits inside a modern broadcast environment — combining traditional DJ technique with multi-camera video, professional audio, live presentation and social-first distribution.
