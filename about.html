Just:DJ

Rick Duncan / Just:DJ

Rick is not being presented as a retro DJ. He is a current electronic-music DJ who chooses turntables as his performance interface. DVS provides access to modern music while retaining traditional manual control.

The Story

The story

Rick Duncan has spent more than three decades mixing on turntables. Just:DJ is the current chapter of that career — a vinyl-powered DJ and broadcast project built to make the DJ performance itself visible and engaging, while presenting it through a modern broadcast and visual format.

The Turntables

The turntables

Turntables are not a visual prop — they are the performance interface. Rick uses Technics turntables with vinyl and DVS/control vinyl, combining the physical feel and manual control of traditional DJing with access to a modern digital catalogue.

Pitch, timing, phrasing, scratching and record control remain hands-on throughout. The technology expands the music library without replacing the DJ technique.

  • Technics turntables
  • Vinyl / control vinyl
  • Serato DVS
  • Manual mixing
  • No sync-led performance

The Music

The music

The music moves across house, techno, trance, drum & bass, jungle, hardcore and the spaces between them. The common thread is energy, strong programming and a DJ performance that's as interesting to watch as it is to hear.

House • Techno • Trance • Jungle • Drum & Bass • Hardcore • Classics

The Broadcast

The broadcast

Just:DJ is produced as a complete live programme, not simply a camera pointed at a DJ booth — multi-camera video, professional audio, live presentation and social-first distribution across landscape and vertical formats.

The Approach

The approach

Modern music. Traditional DJ interface. Modern production. The purpose of Just:DJ is to make the DJ performance itself visible and engaging — hands-on, unmistakably a DJ at work, presented through a broadcast-grade production that respects both the music and the audience watching.
